From SDLC to AIDLC

Moving from AI-assisted coding to a governed, agent-enabled engineering lifecycle.

My work in AI-enabled delivery has focused on applying agents across the software lifecycle—not just code generation. Requirements, architecture, planning, development, testing, review, traceability, technical debt, release and operations can increasingly work as connected, governed AI-assisted workflows with engineers remaining accountable for decisions and outcomes.

REQUIREMENTS→ARCHITECT & PLAN→DEVELOP→VERIFY & TEST→RELEASE & DEPLOY→OPERATE→LEARN & IMPROVE ↻
